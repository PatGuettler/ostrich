Full description
Ostrich Dash is a colorful third-person, three-lane endless runner. Guide an ostrich down an ever-changing track as the scenery moves through Classic Stadium, Beach Track, Night Games, Desert Circuit, Snow Games, and Jungle Track.

Swipe left or right to switch lanes, swipe up to jump, and swipe down to duck. Dodge padded hurdles, foam walls, traffic cones, duck-under gates, referee drones, slippery patches, and rival runners.

Collect feathers during each run to unlock four ostrich skins. Build clean-dodge combos, earn biome medals, complete a daily feather challenge, and improve your personal best.

Charge one of four powers as you run: Shield blocks one collision, Magnet pulls nearby feathers toward you, Slow-Mo gives you extra reaction time, and Score Rush builds your combo faster.

On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or A/D to switch lanes, W or Space to jump, S to duck, and E or Q to activate a charged power.
