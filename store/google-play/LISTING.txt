Full description
Ostrich Dash is a three-lane endless runner. You race an ostrich away down the track, switching lanes, jumping, and ducking as the world shifts through Classic Stadium, Beach, Night, Desert, Snow, and Jungle.

Dodge hurdles, walls, cones, drones, slick patches, and rival runners. Charge Shield, Magnet, Slow-Mo, and Score Rush. Earn feathers, unlock skins, and collect biome medals.

On a phone, swipe sideways to change lanes, swipe up to jump, and swipe down to duck. On a keyboard, use A/D or the arrow keys to change lanes, W or Space to jump, and S to duck.

Free to play. Contains ads.
