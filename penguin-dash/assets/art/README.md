# Penguin Dash Art Set

These production PNGs were created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool and integrated into Godot as project-local assets.

## Direction

Premium children's picture-book illustration combining opaque gouache, watercolor blooms, tactile paper grain, softly imperfect hand-painted edges, and crisp midnight-navy accents. The shared palette is midnight navy, glacier blue, ice cyan, snow cream, ember orange, and gold.

## Prompt set

- `alpine_background.png` — portrait Alpine night environment with painted aurora, layered snowy mountains, warm miniature venue lights, and a quiet central gameplay corridor.
- `penguin_headfirst.png` — active transparent mascot sprite, aligned vertically with its head leading toward the mountain horizon and its shorts, scarf, and feet trailing behind. The playable 3D character is the Meshy Joy biped at `res://assets/models/penguin_joy_biped.glb`, posed belly-first on the chute.
- `penguin_slide.png` — original sideways mascot exploration, retained as a source-art variant but no longer used by the game.
- `sea_lion_lunge.png` — transparent mischievous baby sea-lion lunge sprite matching the penguin's painterly finish and readable obstacle silhouette.
- `golden_fish.png` — transparent round gold fish collectible with glossy eye, rosy cheek, smiling mouth, cyan highlights, and a heart-like tail.
- `ice_block.png` — transparent low cluster of hand-painted translucent cyan ice crystals with cream frost and gold glints.
- `race_gate.png` — transparent carved-ice Winter Games gate with orange/gold chevrons, frost caps, and cyan ribbons.
- `track_ice.png` — archived track-material exploration. It is intentionally not rendered: `alpine_background.png` is now the playable mountain slide.
- `title_crest.png` — transparent carved-ice oval title medallion with painted snow, cyan/gold rays, heart and snowflake carvings, and an orange flourish.

Every cutout prompt required genuine transparent alpha, no text, no logo, no watermark, no scenery, and a production-ready silhouette. The environment and surface prompts required no characters or UI. Generated outputs were visually checked in the real 540×960 Godot viewport after integration.
